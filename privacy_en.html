Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-07 · Holographic Cosmos · Provided by jungyong kim

1. Information we collect: none. The app does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data or any other personal information.

2. Internet use: none. All data and screens are bundled inside the app. It sends no information to any server while running. (The Mac version's sandbox settings include a network permission only so the web-view engine can start; the app itself does not communicate.)

3. Stored on your device: only your display language (Korean/English) and your ambient-sound on/off setting. These values never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

4. Third parties / ads / tracking: none. There are no ads, analytics tools, third-party SDKs or user tracking.

5. Purchases and payments: downloads and payments are handled by Apple; we do not receive purchasers' personal information. Apple provides us only aggregate statistics (such as download counts) through App Store Connect that cannot identify individuals.

6. Children: because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

7. This website: the site uses no cookies or analytics. The company hosting the site may keep server access logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy.

8. Access and deletion requests: we hold no personal information, so there is nothing to access or delete. You are still welcome to contact us with questions.

9. Changes: if a future version adds a feature that uses the internet (for example live launch schedules), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before release and post the new effective date here.

10. Contact: britkocosmosinitiative@gmail.com
