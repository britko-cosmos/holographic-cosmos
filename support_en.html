Quick start

Tap an object to bring it to the center and open its info panel. The round buttons on the right control zoom, pause, sound (SND) and speed (2X–128X); the buttons to their left show or hide the ISS, Starlink, James Webb and the fictional station. The ☰ button at the bottom opens the 12 categories and the box beside it searches by name. Tap "View in detail" next to a name to enter that star system.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?
No. All data is inside the app, so it works in airplane mode.

Are the positions accurate? Is it live tracking?
Planet positions and orbital/rotation speeds are based on real astronomical data (NASA/JPL approximate orbital elements and others) and start at today's real positions. The ISS, Starlink and Webb are estimates computed from orbital elements, not live tracking. Some sizes, distances and nebula shapes are approximated or simplified.

The ISS or Starbase looks the wrong size.
Facilities near Earth would be invisible if drawn at true scale next to the planet, so they are drawn oversized to be visible. The info panel says so.

What is the object marked "fictional"?
A single fictional station, an original creative addition; it is not a real object. Its info panel marks it as fictional.

Is the Starbase launch schedule live?
No. The flight record in the panel is a dated snapshot from when it was checked. Tap "Official schedule" in the panel to see the latest on SpaceX's official page (it opens your browser).

I want to turn the sound off.
Tap SND among the round buttons. The setting is remembered.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data collected?
No. See the Privacy Policy — nothing is collected.
