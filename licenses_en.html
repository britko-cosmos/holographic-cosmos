Natural Earth

Source of the Earth outline data. It is in the public domain and needs no attribution, but we credit it with thanks. naturalearthdata.com

Astronomical data

Planet positions: NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics approximate orbital elements (Standish) and Meeus' lunar formulas. Directions and distances of nearby stars, clusters and nebulae are approximations compiled from public astronomical catalogs and references. Background stars and nebula colors are procedurally generated.
