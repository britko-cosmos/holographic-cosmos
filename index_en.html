Today, the real Solar System

From Mercury to Neptune, everything starts at its real position for today's date and moves in real time at its real orbital and rotation periods (sidereal days). Earth's day/night line and the Moon's phase match today too. Speeds run from 2X to 128X with pause, and satellites or telescopes you've switched off keep moving at their real periods while hidden.

Features

Solar System

8 planets, dwarf planet Pluto, 18 moons

True distance and size ratios, Lagrange points (L1–L5) and Trojan regions. Every planet gets the hologram grid and day/night shading toward the real Sun.

Around Earth

ISS · Starlink · Webb · Starbase

The International Space Station, the Starlink swarm, the James Webb Space Telescope on its Sun–Earth L2 halo orbit (about 3.9× farther than the Moon), and the Starbase launch site in Texas with a Starship model.

Deep Space

22+ galaxies

From the LMC, SMC, Andromeda and Triangulum to dwarf galaxies, at their real directions, distances and shapes.

Neighborhood

Nearby stars and exoplanet systems

Proxima Centauri, TRAPPIST-1, Sirius and other nearby stars appear in their real directions; 10 constellations plus nebulae and clusters use real coordinates.

Explore

Categories & search

Browse 12 categories — clusters, nebulae, galaxies, star systems, planets, moons, satellites, space stations, telescopes and more — or search by name and jump straight there.

Local Group

The whole Local Group

22 galaxies around the Milky Way, with trail lines showing their real motion.

Privacy & internet

No ads, no sign-up, no analytics. All data lives inside the app, so it works in airplane mode and collects no personal information. See the Privacy Policy.

Models of facilities near Earth are simplified and drawn oversized so they are visible. One fictional station is included and clearly marked as fictional.
